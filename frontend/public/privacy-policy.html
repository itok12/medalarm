MedAlarm Privacy Policy

MedAlarm is a medication reminder app. This policy explains what data it handles, why, who else processes it, how long it is kept, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is [TO FILL: your full legal name], an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, trading as MedAlarm. Contact: support@medalarmapp.com.

Two ways to use MedAlarm

Without an account (guest mode). Everything you enter stays on your phone. MedAlarm's servers never receive your medicines, reminders or dose history. Removing the app, or choosing "Delete" when asked about guest data, erases it.

With an account. Your data is stored on MedAlarm's servers so it can sync and be shared with a caregiver you approve. You must agree to this when you create the account (see "Health data and your consent").

Health data and your consent

Your medicines and dose history are health data, a special category of personal data. We only store it on our servers with your explicit consent, which you give when you create an account (or, for accounts created before October 2026, the first time you open the app after this update). We record when you agreed and which version of the wording you agreed to.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see below). Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (you can also export your dose history as a CSV file from the History screen),
  • correct it (most of it you can edit in the app),
  • delete it (Profile ▸ Delete account in the app, or https://medalarmapp.com/delete-account.html),
  • restrict or object to how we use it,
  • move it to another service.

Email support@medalarmapp.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as hashes. Caregivers see nothing until you approve them. On your phone, your sign-in is kept in app-private storage.

Children

MedAlarm is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

MedAlarm is a reminder tool, not medical advice, and is not a medical device. Follow your doctor or pharmacist's instructions. Reminders depend on your phone's settings and can be delayed, so don't rely on MedAlarm alone for medicines where a missed or late dose is dangerous.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. If a change affects how we use your health data, we will ask for your consent again in the app.
