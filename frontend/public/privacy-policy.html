MedAlarm Privacy Policy

MedAlarm stores the account information, medicine schedules, reminder preferences, and adherence history needed to deliver the app experience.

What MedAlarm stores

  • Account details such as username, email address, password hash, timezone, and reminder defaults.
  • Medicines, alarms, adherence logs, and caregiver links that you explicitly create.
  • Optional telemetry for analytics and crash reporting when deployment-level environment variables enable those integrations.

What MedAlarm does not do

  • Sell personal data.
  • Share your adherence history with other users by default.
  • Allow caregivers to edit your medicines or alarms in this version.

Questions

If you need help or want to request data changes, use the support page or the account deletion page available on the deployed MedAlarm site.
