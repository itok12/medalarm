Delete your MedAlarm account

Use this page to start a permanent MedAlarm account deletion request outside the mobile app, as required by Google Play policy.

What will be deleted

  • Your MedAlarm account profile.
  • Medicine schedules, alarms, and adherence history linked to that account.
  • Caregiver relationships connected to that account.

How to complete the request

  1. Open the secure deletion page.
  2. Sign in with the MedAlarm account you want to delete.
  3. Enter your current password and confirm the deletion request.

Need help first?

If you are trying to change reminder settings or update your email, use the MedAlarm profile and settings pages instead of deleting the whole account.
