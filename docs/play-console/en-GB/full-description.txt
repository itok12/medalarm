MedAlarm is a medication reminder and adherence tracking app built for everyday routines.

Set up medicines, generate reminders from your schedule, and log doses as taken, skipped, or snoozed in seconds.

MedAlarm helps you:
- manage medicines with start and end dates
- generate reminders from your routine
- review adherence history over time
- use a focused Today screen with quick actions
- share read-only adherence visibility with linked caregivers

The Android app uses native local notifications for reliable reminders, while the web app stays installable as a PWA.

MedAlarm is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or professional medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional.
