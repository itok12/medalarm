MedAlarm is a medicine reminder app built for real routines.

Set up medicines, generate alarms from your schedule, and log doses as taken, skipped, or snoozed in seconds.

MedAlarm helps you:
- manage medicines with start and end dates
- generate reminders from your routine
- review adherence history over time
- use a focused Today screen with quick actions
- share read-only adherence visibility with linked caregivers

The Android app uses native local notifications for reliable reminders, while the web app stays installable as a PWA.
