MedAlarm reminds you to take your medicines and keeps a simple record of every dose.

Start straight away, no account needed: in guest mode everything stays on your phone. Create a free account later if you want your medicines to sync, and bring your saved data with you in one tap.

What MedAlarm does:
- Reminds you at each dose time, then nudges you again 15 and 30 minutes later if you haven't logged it
- Lets you mark a dose Taken or Snooze it right from the notification
- Shows today's doses at a glance, with what you've already taken and an Undo if you tap by mistake
- Counts your pills and warns you before you run out
- Keeps your dose history, which you can export as a CSV file to share with your doctor or pharmacist
- Lets a family member or carer follow your doses, only after you approve their request, and you can stop sharing at any time
- Checks your phone's settings and tells you what to fix so reminders arrive on time, with a test reminder to prove it
- Works with large text sizes and screen readers

Your privacy: guest data never leaves your phone. With an account, your medicines are stored only with your explicit consent, never sold, and never used for advertising. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app.

For on-time reminders, allow "Alarms & reminders" when MedAlarm asks. Without it, Android may deliver reminders late.

MedAlarm is a reminder tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor or pharmacist's instructions. Don't rely on MedAlarm alone for medicines where a missed or late dose is dangerous.
