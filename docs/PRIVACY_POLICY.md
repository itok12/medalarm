# MedAlarm Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026

MedAlarm is a medication reminder app. This policy explains what data it
handles, why, who else processes it, how long it is kept, and your rights
under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

## Two ways to use MedAlarm

**Without an account (guest mode).** Everything you enter stays on your
phone. MedAlarm's servers never receive your medicines, reminders or dose
history. Removing the app, or choosing "Delete" when asked about guest data,
erases it.

**With an account.** Your data is stored on MedAlarm's servers so it can sync
and be shared with a caregiver you approve. You must agree to this when you
create the account (see "Health data and your consent").

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Lawful basis |
|------|-----|--------------|
| Username, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), timezone, default alarm time, email-reminder preference | To run your account and sign you in | Contract (Article 6(1)(b)) |
| Medicines (name, dose, frequency, instructions, start/end dates, pill counts), reminder times, and dose history (taken, skipped, snoozed, with times) | To schedule reminders, sync them, and show your history | Your **explicit consent** (Article 9(2)(a)), plus contract |
| Caregiver links: who asked to see your history, and whether you approved | To share your history only with people you approve | Your consent, given by approving each request |
| A record each time a caregiver views your history | So you can see and audit access | Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) |
| The photo you take when you use "Scan label" | To read the medicine name and dose from the label; it is not stored | Your explicit consent, given each time you take a scan |
| Your IP address and the account name you try to sign in with, held in memory for up to 15 minutes | To block password-guessing and spam sign-ups | Legitimate interests |

MedAlarm does not use advertising, does not sell data, and does not use
analytics or crash-reporting services at present. If that changes, this
policy will be updated first.

## Health data and your consent

Your medicines and dose history are health data, a special category of
personal data. We only store it on our servers with your explicit consent,
which you give when you create an account (or, for accounts created before
October 2026, the first time you open the app after this update). We record
when you agreed and which version of the wording you agreed to.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see below).
Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew.

## Who else processes your data

We use these service providers. They act only on our instructions.

| Provider | What they do | Location |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| Render Services, Inc. | Hosts the MedAlarm servers and database | United States |
| Anthropic, PBC | Reads medicine labels from photos you choose to scan | United States |

Caregivers you approve can read your dose history (medicine names, times,
taken or skipped). They cannot change your medicines or reminders.

We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## International transfers

Our hosting provider and label-scan provider are in the United States. These
transfers are protected by each provider's Data Processing Addendum:

- **Render** transfers data under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy
  Framework, or, where that does not apply, the UK International Data Transfer
  Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.
- **Anthropic** transfers data under the UK International Data Transfer
  Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep data

- **Account and health data:** while your account exists. When you delete
  your account, it is removed from our live database straight away and from
  any database backups within 7 days, when those backups expire.
- **Scanned label photos:** not stored by MedAlarm. Anthropic processes them
  under its own API terms.
- **Sign-in protection data:** up to 15 minutes, in memory only.
- **Guest-mode data:** on your phone only, until you delete it or remove the app.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your data (you can also export your dose history as a
  CSV file from the History screen),
- correct it (most of it you can edit in the app),
- delete it (Profile ▸ Delete account in the app, or
  https://medalarmapp.com/delete-account.html),
- restrict or object to how we use it,
- move it to another service.

Email support@medalarmapp.com and we will reply within one month. If you are
unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

## Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only
as hashes. Caregivers see nothing until you approve them. On your phone, your
sign-in is kept in app-private storage.

## Children

MedAlarm is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Not medical advice

MedAlarm is a reminder tool, not medical advice, and is not a medical device.
Follow your doctor or pharmacist's instructions. Reminders depend on your
phone's settings and can be delayed, so don't rely on MedAlarm alone for
medicines where a missed or late dose is dangerous.

## Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. If a change
affects how we use your health data, we will ask for your consent again in
the app.
